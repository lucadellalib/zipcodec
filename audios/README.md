# Sample Audio Attribution

This directory contains short speech samples for experimenting with ZipCodec
resynthesis. The samples originate from the datasets below and remain subject
to their respective dataset licenses. The repository's Apache-2.0 license does
not replace or override those terms.

| `english/p*.wav` | English | [VoiceBank noisy speech database](https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2791) | Test set | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

## Dataset Citations

### LibriSpeech

Vassil Panayotov, Guoguo Chen, Daniel Povey, and Sanjeev Khudanpur,
“LibriSpeech: An ASR Corpus Based on Public Domain Audio Books,” ICASSP 2015.

### VoiceBank

Cassia Valentini-Botinhao, “Noisy speech database for training speech
enhancement algorithms and TTS models,” University of Edinburgh, 2017,
[doi:10.7488/ds/2117](https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2117).

### Multilingual LibriSpeech

Vineel Pratap, Qiantong Xu, Anuroop Sriram, Gabriel Synnaeve, and Ronan
Collobert, “MLS: A Large-Scale Multilingual Dataset for Speech Research,”
Interspeech 2020.

### MAGICDATA

Magic Data Technology Co., Ltd., “MAGICDATA Mandarin Chinese Read Speech
Corpus,” May 2019.

MAGICDATA is restricted to non-commercial use and does not permit distribution
of derivatives. Consult the source license before reusing its sample.
